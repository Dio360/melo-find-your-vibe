# **1\. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Melo is an African-first music streaming and discovery platform designed to help people discover, understand and enjoy music through:

* Vibe  
* Culture  
* Personalization  
* African discovery  
* Artificial intelligence  
* Affordability  
* Data-conscious listening

Melo is not intended to compete primarily by having the largest catalogue.

Instead, Melo is designed around a more focused proposition:

> **Help users find the right music faster, especially when they don't know exactly what they want to search for.**

A user should be able to open Melo and say:

> "I'm studying tonight. Give me calm Nigerian music that won't distract me."

Or:

> "Give me something like Asake, but introduce me to artists I probably don't know."

Or:

> "I want something for a Lagos night drive."

Melo should translate these requests into relevant music discovery using:

* Catalogue metadata  
* Vibe intelligence  
* Search  
* Personalization  
* Recommendation systems  
* User behaviour  
* African cultural context  
* AI-powered natural-language understanding

The core experience is:

**Find Your Vibe → Discover → Listen → Save → Melo Learns → Discover Better → Return**

# **3\. PRODUCT POSITIONING**

## **Melo is**

An African-first music streaming and discovery platform that combines:

**Streaming \+ African Discovery \+ Vibe Intelligence \+ AI \+ Personalization \+ Data-Conscious Listening**

## **Melo is not**

Melo should not initially attempt to become:

* A Spotify clone  
* A YouTube Music clone  
* A social network  
* A full artist-management platform  
* A podcast-first platform  
* A general-purpose AI assistant  
* A complex creator ecosystem

Those may become future capabilities.

The first job is:

> **Help people discover and enjoy music they love.**

# **4\. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES**

## **4.1 Africa-first, not Africa-only**

African music should be central to Melo's identity without preventing users from discovering global music.

## **4.2 Discovery over catalogue size**

Melo should compete through the quality of discovery and personalization rather than catalogue-size claims.

## **4.3 Vibe before complexity**

Users should not need to understand genres or music terminology before they can discover something they enjoy.

## **4.4 AI assists discovery**

AI should make discovery easier.

AI should not replace:

* Human curiosity  
* Editorial discovery  
* Artist discovery  
* User control  
* Catalogue truth

## **4.5 Data-conscious by design**

Data consumption is part of the product experience.

Users should be able to control:

* Streaming quality  
* Data usage  
* Autoplay behaviour  
* Download behaviour  
* Wi-Fi/mobile-data preferences

## **4.6 Local culture matters**

Nigerian:

* Languages  
* Regions  
* Genres  
* Artists  
* Cultural contexts  
* Listening behaviours  
* Everyday situations

should be represented naturally.

## **4.7 Fast and simple**

Melo should remain usable under imperfect network conditions.

## **4.8 Never invent catalogue information**

AI must not fabricate:

* Artists  
* Songs  
* Albums  
* Playlists  
* Lyrics  
* Release information  
* Catalogue availability

Every playable recommendation must resolve to a valid catalogue entity or an explicitly approved external source.

## **4.9 Rights before scale**

No production music catalogue should be streamed until the required rights and commercial permissions have been established.

# **19\. AI SAFETY AND QUALITY RULES**

AI must:

* Never fabricate catalogue entities.  
* Never claim a track exists when it doesn't.  
* Never claim a track is playable when it isn't.  
* Respect explicit/clean preferences.  
* Respect territory availability.  
* Respect subscription entitlement.  
* Respect rights restrictions.  
* Use structured catalogue tools.  
* Log AI request outcomes for quality analysis.  
* Provide graceful fallback recommendations.

If no exact match exists:

> "I couldn't find an exact match, but these are close to the vibe you're describing."

# **24\. PLAYBACK ARCHITECTURE**

The application server should not be the primary audio streaming server.

Architecture:

Melo Client  
     ↓  
Playback Authorization  
     ↓  
Short-lived / signed media access  
     ↓  
CDN / Edge  
     ↓  
Audio Asset  
     ↓  
Melo Player

Playback authorization should validate:

* User identity  
* Track availability  
* Territory  
* Rights  
* Subscription entitlement  
* Device/session rules where applicable

Audio URLs should not become permanently reusable public URLs.

# **35\. CONTENT RIGHTS AND LICENSING**

## **CRITICAL RELEASE GATE**

Production Melo must not stream commercial music without appropriate rights.

Rights work must cover, where applicable:

* Musical works  
* Sound recordings  
* Albums  
* Lyrics  
* Podcasts  
* DJ mixes  
* Mixtapes  
* Downloads  
* Offline playback  
* Territory  
* Commercial use  
* Advertising-supported use  
* Subscription-supported use

The prototype may use:

* Mock tracks  
* Licensed test content  
* Royalty-cleared audio  
* Officially authorized demo/test sources

The phrase "approved public/demo streaming sources" must not be interpreted as permission to use arbitrary public audio.

Production catalogue sources must be explicitly authorized for Melo's intended use.

# **98\. API IDEMPOTENCY**

Important operations should support idempotency where applicable:

* Payment initiation  
* Payment webhook processing  
* Playlist creation where retries can occur  
* Likes  
* Subscription updates  
* Download requests

This reduces duplicate operations caused by network retries.

---

# **99\. DATABASE PRINCIPLES**

PostgreSQL should remain the primary source of truth for transactional data.

Redis should be treated as a cache or short-lived state layer rather than the primary permanent data store.

Analytics data can later be moved into an analytics warehouse or specialized event system when scale justifies it.

---

# **100\. SEARCH SCALE STRATEGY**

Do not introduce OpenSearch/Elasticsearch simply because it is technically available.

Start with PostgreSQL.

Move to a dedicated search engine when:

* Catalogue scale demands it  
* Search relevance becomes difficult to manage  
* Search analytics show measurable limitations  
* Advanced discovery requires it

# **101\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — MODULAR MONOLITH**

This decision is retained.

Reasons:

* Faster initial development  
* Lower operational complexity  
* Easier debugging  
* Easier local development  
* Strong domain boundaries  
* Future extraction remains possible

Melo should avoid premature microservices.

---

# **102\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — AI PLATFORM LAYER**

AI must remain replaceable.

The system should allow Melo to change:

* LLM provider  
* Model  
* Prompting  
* Embeddings  
* Ranking logic  
* Recommendation models

without rewriting the product.

---

# **103\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — PAYMENT ABSTRACTION**

Payment provider integrations should sit behind a Melo payment interface.

This supports:

* Provider switching  
* Redundancy  
* Different market requirements  
* Payment-channel differences  
* Future African expansion

---

# **104\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — AUDIO DELIVERY SEPARATION**

Audio delivery remains separate from the application server.

Application  
    ↓  
Playback Authorization  
    ↓  
CDN  
    ↓  
Audio

This allows the application backend to focus on business logic rather than becoming the primary bandwidth bottleneck.

---

# **105\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — RIGHTS AS A DOMAIN**

Rights should not be treated as a spreadsheet or legal document outside the product.

Rights affect:

* Search visibility  
* Playback  
* Downloads  
* Offline  
* Territory  
* Subscription  
* Advertising  
* AI recommendations

Therefore:

> **Rights are part of the product architecture.**

# **106\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — ENTITLEMENT AS A DOMAIN**

Melo should separate:

 ↓  
Subscription  
 ↓  
Entitlement

Feature Access

# **107\. ARCHITECTURE DECISION — DATA SAVER AS CORE PRODUCT**
